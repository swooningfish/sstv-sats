Receiving tips

  • Modes in use: Robot 36, Robot 72, Martin M1 and PD120/PD180. Decoders read the VIS code at the start of each picture and pick the mode automatically.
  • On a Quansheng UV-K5 or UV-K1, the F4WHN custom firmware's SSTV app decodes pictures on the radio itself.
  • FM, wide filter if your radio has one. Squelch open, volume steady, and feed the audio to MMSSTV or RX-SSTV (Windows), QSSTV (Linux), Black Cat SSTV (macOS) or Robot36 (Android).
  • Passes above 30° give the best pictures. A handheld Yagi beats a rubber duck by a mile.
  • These satellites send SSTV periodically, not all the time. Record the whole pass and decode it afterwards.
  • Satellite list from LU3FCI, Juan Carlos. 73 and good reception!
